04: 1. GENERAL:
05: The broad guidelines to the preparation of B.E. / B.Tech. report are outlined below.
06: 2. NUMBER OF COPIES TO BE SUBMITTED FOR EVALUATION:
07: B.E. / B.Tech. Report: The number copies to be submitted to the Department where the student is
08: studying is No. of students in the project batch / group plus two (one copy each to student(s), one
09: copy each to the supervisor and department library). The Report shall also be submitted in soft
10: copy to the Department.
11: 3. SIZE OF REPORT:
12: The size of the project report should be sixty (60) pages (+/- 10%) of typed matter reckoned from
13: the first page of Chapter 1 to the last page including the list of References.
14: 4. ARRANGEMENT OF CONTENTS OF REPORT:
15: The sequence in which the report material should be arranged and bound should be as follows:
16: 1. Cover page & Title page
17: 2. Bonafide Certificate
18: 3. Abstract
19: 4. Acknowledgement
20: 5. Table of Contents
21: 6. List of Tables
22: 7. List of Figures
23: 8. List of Symbols, Abbreviations and Nomenclature
24: 9. Chapters
25: 10. Appendices
26: 11. References
27: The Tables and Figures shall be introduced in the appropriate places.

19: punctuation and centered at the top of the page. The text should commence 2 spaces below this
20: heading.

28: the student. The term ‘SUPERVISOR’ must be typed in capital letters between the

22: further divided into several divisions and sub-divisions.

27: directly underneath in the very same page, which refers to the material they annotate.

29: included in the main text may serve as a distraction and cloud the central theme under
30: discussion.

35: (cid:1) Appendices shall carry the title of the work reported and the same title shall be made

02: 7.2 Chapters
03: The format for typing chapter headings, division headings and sub division headings shall be
04: same as given in Table of Contents.
05: The word CHAPTER without punctuation should be centred 50mm down from the top of the
06: page. Two spaces below, the title of the chapter should be typed centrally in capital letters.
07: The text should commence 4 spaces below this title, the first letter of the text starting 20mm,
08: inside from the left hand margin.
09: The division and sub-division captions along with their numberings should be left-justified.
10: The captions should start at 20mm from left margin. The typed material directly below
11: division or sub-division heading should commence 2 spaces below it and should be offset
12: 20mm from the left hand margin. Within a division or sub-division paragraphs are permitted.
13: The paragraph should commence 3 spaces below the last line of the preceding paragraph or
14: caption, the first letter in the paragraph being offset from the left hand margin by 20mm.
15: 8. NUMBERING INSTRUCTIONS
16: 8.1 Page Numbering
17: All pages numbers (whether it be in Roman or Arabic numbers) should be typed without
18: punctuation on the upper right hand corner 20mm from top with the last digit in line with the
19: right hand margin. The preliminary pages of the project report (such as Title page,
20: Acknowledgement, Table of Contents etc.) should be numbered in lower case Roman
21: numerals. The title page will be numbered as (i) but this should not be typed. The page
22: immediately following the title page shall be numbered (ii) and it should appear at the top
23: right hand corner as already specified. Pages of main text, starting with Chapter 1 should be
24: consecutively numbered using Arabic numerals.
25: 8.2 Numbering of Chapters, Divisions and Sub-Divisions
26: The numbering of chapters, divisions and sub-divisions should be done using Arabic
27: numerals only and further decimal notation should be used for numbering the divisions and
28: sub-divisions within a chapter. For example, division / sub-division 4 under division 3
29: belonging to chapter 2 should be numbered as 2.3.4. The title for the division/sub-division
30: shall start at 20mm from the left margin, following the number assigned to it.
31: Every chapter beginning with the first chapter should be serially numbered using Arabic
32: numerals. Appendices included should also be numbered in an identical manner starting with
33: Appendix 1.

02: 8.3 Numbering of Tables and Figures
03: Tables and Figures appearing anywhere in the project report should bear appropriate numbers.
04: The rule for assigning such numbers is illustrated through an example. Thus, if a figure in
05: Chapter 3, happens to be the fourth then assign 3.4 to that figure. Identical rules apply for
06: tables except that the word Figure is replaced by the word Table. If figures (or tables) appear
07: in appendices then figure 3 in Appendix 2 will be designated as Figure A 2.3. If a table to be
08: continued into the next page this may be done, but no line should be drawn underneath an
09: unfinished table. A table continued into the next page should have a caption like, Table 2.1
10: (continued), placed centrally and underlined.
11: 8.4 Numbering of Equations
12: Equations appearing in each Chapter or Appendix should be numbered serially, the
13: numbering shall commence freshly for each Chapter or Appendix. Thus for example, an
14: equation appearing in Chapter 2, if it happens to be the eighth equation in that Chapter should
15: be numbered (2.8), while referring to this equation in the body of the project report it
16: should be referred to as Equation (2.8).
